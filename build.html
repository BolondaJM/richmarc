Why one team

Fewer handoffs, fewer surprises

When the architect, interior designer and builder sit at the same table, decisions get made once. Drawings are priced while they are drawn, so the house you approve is the house you can afford.

  • Site study covering soil, slope, sun path and planning controls
  • 3D walkthroughs of every room before construction starts
  • Fixed price contract once drawings are approved
  • Client portal with weekly photo reports and live schedule
The build journey

Five stages, one team, no guesswork

  1. 01

    Discovery consultation

    We learn how you live, what the land allows and what you want to spend. You leave with a clear recommendation and a rough budget range.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Concept design

    Floor plans, massing and a material palette, developed over two or three working sessions with our architects.

    Weeks 2 to 8
  3. 03

    Detailed design and fixed price

    Full drawings, interior selections and engineering. You sign one fixed price contract covering everything you approved.

    Months 3 to 5
  4. 04

    Construction

    One site manager from slab to final coat, with weekly photo reports and site visits whenever you want them.

    Months 6 to 18
  5. 05

    Handover and aftercare

    A guided walkthrough of every system in the house, then return visits at 3 and 12 months to check and adjust.

    Ongoing
